Mathematical Formulation of the Adversarial Objective

The fundamental two-player zero-sum game formulated by Goodfellow et al. (2014) and Radford et al. (2015).

min_G max_D V(D, G) = E_{x ~ p_data}[log D(x)] + E_{z ~ p_z}[log(1 - D(G(z)))]

Variable Definitions & Theoretical Roles:

  • G (Generator): Neural network parameterized by θg that maps a random latent prior z ~ N(0, I) ∈ R100 into synthetic pixel space G(z) ∈ [-1, 1]3×64×64.
  • D (Discriminator): Convolutional binary classifier parameterized by θd that outputs a scalar probability D(x) ∈ [0, 1] indicating whether image x was sampled from the true CelebA dataset distribution pdata rather than the generator distribution pg.
  • Ex ~ p_data[log D(x)]: Expected log-likelihood that the discriminator correctly labels genuine face images as real (label = 1.0).
  • Ez ~ p_z[log(1 - D(G(z)))]: Expected log-likelihood that the discriminator correctly rejects synthetic images generated from noise vector z (label = 0.0).
  • Non-Saturating Generator Cost: In practice, rather than minimizing log(1 - D(G(z))) which suffers from vanishing gradients early in training, the generator is trained to maximize log D(G(z)).
